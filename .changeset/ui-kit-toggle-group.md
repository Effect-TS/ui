Add `ToggleGroup`, a row of toggle buttons that share one value: the values of the pressed items, one at a time or several with `multiple`. It is a headless component with a `root` and an `item` part. Only one item is in the Tab order, and the arrow keys of its `orientation`, Home and End move focus between the enabled items, wrapping with `loopFocus` and following a `DirectionProvider`.

Add `ToastProvider`, a headless toast stack. Render it once near the root with a `view` closure that holds the app's body and the toasts, drawn with `toastProvider.toasts` and the parts `viewport`, `root`, `content`, `title`, `description`, `action` and `close`. Every part but the viewport takes `{ element, toast }`. Any component inside the closure posts with `$.send(ToastProvider, { _tag: "AddedToast", toast })`, and updates or closes a toast by its `id` with `UpdatedToast` and `ClosedToast`. The provider keeps the list, so a closing toast keeps its content until its exit transition ends. A toast closes after its `timeout` (5000 ms by default, `0` or `type: "loading"` to keep it), and every timer pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is in the stack or the window is blurred. Toasts past `limit` (3 by default) are marked `data-limited` and made `inert`. F6 moves focus into the stack and back, Escape closes the focused toast, and focus moves to the next toast when a focused one closes. The provider emits `Dismissed` when the user closes a toast or its timeout runs out.

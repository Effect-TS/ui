DevTools can stop recording a message `_tag` in one component, every instance of a component, or an instance subtree, from the Stop recording menu, `exclude` or the `set_recording` tool: future matching entries take no buffer slots, and rows already buffered are hidden until recording resumes. `PropsChanged` entries now appear under Everything instead of Messages.

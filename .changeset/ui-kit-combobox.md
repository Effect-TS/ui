Add `Combobox`, a headless combobox ported from Base UI. Call it as `Combobox(h, { ... })` with `items`, the options with their labels, and apply `combobox.input`, `combobox.positioner`, `combobox.popup`, `combobox.list`, `combobox.item` and the other parts to your own builders. `open`, `value` and `inputValue` are controlled: the combobox emits `Opened`, `Dismissed`, `Picked` and `EditedInput`, each with a reason, `Highlighted` when the highlighted item changes, and `Settled` when its transitions end. The combobox filters `items` by the text, with `filter` set to `"contains"`, `"startsWith"`, `"endsWith"` or `"none"`, or takes your own results as `filteredItems`, and hands back `combobox.items` to draw. `makeComboboxFilter` exports its matchers. Focus stays in the input while the arrow keys move a highlight through the list. `multiple` selects several, drawn as chips; `inputInPopup` puts the input inside the popup, with the trigger as the combobox; `grid` lays the items out in rows; `inline` draws the list without a popup; and `virtualized` lets you draw a slice of the list. `name`, `required` and `autoComplete` add a hidden input next to the input, so a required combobox blocks its form's submission. In a `Field`, draw the input as `combobox.input(field.control(h.input))`.

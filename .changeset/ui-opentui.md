Add `@effect/ui-opentui`, an OpenTUI vocabulary, terminal host, global Subscriptions, imperative Commands, and test harnesses (`TestOpenTuiEvents` and `OpenTuiTest`) for building terminal apps with `@effect/ui`.

Add `Subscription.direct`, `View.withNoTextContent`, and `View.WithTags` to `@effect/ui` so external hosts and strict-text vocabularies with their own `text` tag integrate cleanly without touching internal APIs.

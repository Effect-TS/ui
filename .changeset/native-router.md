Add `NativeRouter`, which runs the `Router` on a history in memory with Android Back and deep links, and the kit's `Stack` and `Link`. Covered screens stay mounted and take no Back presses, and the screen reader's focus moves to the screen that comes to the top. `StackSlide` adds an opt-in push and pop transition on the native driver, and `StackSwipe` adds iOS swipe-back.
